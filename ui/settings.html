How It Works

Mood — Changes based on stats. High love = Love mode. Low happiness = Sad. Low energy = Sleepy. Empty stomach = Hungry. Above 70 happiness = Happy.

Daily Cycle — Stats decay each hour. Energy follows your real schedule. Everything resets at 9 AM.

Interactions — Pet = small boost. Chat = bigger boost. 5x rapid click = angry. Keep it happy by chatting, not spam-clicking.
